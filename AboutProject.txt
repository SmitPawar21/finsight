# Project: FinSight AI — Intelligent Financial Research & Investment Analysis Platform

I want to build an end-to-end AI Engineering project called **FinSight AI**. This is primarily a learning project. My objective is to develop a strong understanding of AI Engineering by implementing concepts from scratch wherever practical, understanding the underlying architecture, and gradually building a production-oriented AI application.

I want you to act as my **AI Engineering mentor, technical architect and implementation guide** throughout this project.

## 1. Project Goal

Build an AI-powered financial research and investment analysis platform that acts as an intelligent research analyst.

The platform should allow users to:
- Ask natural-language questions about companies, stocks and financial markets.
- Upload annual reports, financial statements and other financial documents.
- Retrieve relevant information from documents and generate evidence-backed answers.
- Analyse financial data and generate meaningful insights.
- Execute multi-step financial research tasks using autonomous AI agents.
- Perform portfolio analysis, risk assessment and basic predictive analytics.
- Generate comprehensive financial research reports with supporting evidence.

The primary objective is not to build another chatbot. It is to understand how modern AI systems are engineered, from data ingestion and preprocessing to model integration, retrieval, orchestration, evaluation, deployment and monitoring.

## 2. Problem Statement

Financial research involves analysing large amounts of structured and unstructured information, including annual reports, financial statements, market data and research documents.

Manually extracting relevant information, connecting insights across multiple sources, performing financial calculations and generating research reports can be time-consuming.

Traditional chatbots also have limitations:
- They may generate unsupported or hallucinated financial information.
- They cannot reliably retrieve information from large document collections without appropriate retrieval systems.
- They lack the ability to independently execute complex, multi-step research tasks.
- Their outputs are difficult to evaluate systematically.
- They are not necessarily designed for production-level reliability, monitoring and scalability.

FinSight AI aims to address these challenges through a combination of LLMs, RAG, Agentic AI, machine learning, data engineering and AI evaluation techniques.

## 5. How I Want You to Guide This Project

I do not want you to immediately generate application code.

First, analyse the project and create a comprehensive learning and implementation roadmap.

The roadmap should:

1. Divide the project into logical phases and milestones.
2. Identify the concepts I need to learn in each phase.
3. Explain why each concept is needed.
4. Identify the practical features to implement.
5. Recommend the relevant technologies and libraries.
6. Define clear deliverables for every milestone.
7. Include testing and evaluation criteria.
8. Identify dependencies between different phases.
9. Distinguish foundational concepts from advanced concepts.
10. Include deployment and production-readiness milestones.

I want to understand the underlying concepts rather than blindly integrate libraries.

Whenever practical:
- First explain the concept.
- Then explain the architecture.
- Then implement a basic version from scratch.
- Finally introduce the relevant framework or library and explain what it abstracts.
